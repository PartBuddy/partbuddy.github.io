Describe the part. PartBuddy builds it, step by step.

PartBuddy is a free desktop app for AI-assisted parametric design of FDM 3D-print parts. An assistant builds the part in a real CAD kernel while you watch every step in the 3D view, pin notes to faces and edges, and answer clarifying questions. You bring your own model: an existing Claude or ChatGPT subscription, a cloud API key, or a local server.
